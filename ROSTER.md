# Specialist roster

AZRAEL-54 is the product name. There are 66 registered roles: 16 original classifier roles, 23 additional red/blue pairs, HEPHAESTUS, OBSIDIAN, CRUCIBLE and one junior OSINT role.

| Research role | Defensive counterpart |
|---|---|

HEPHAESTUS: authorized adversary research, lab evidence, detection and remediation.

OBSIDIAN: minimize unnecessary exposure and measure what remains; privacy, hardening and accountable audit evidence.

CRUCIBLE is the independent scope and evidence reviewer proposed with HEPHAESTUS and OBSIDIAN. It reports findings to the administrator and has no execution authority.

The 50 new entries are role contracts, not independently trained neural networks. They participate in role selection, shared-SLM context and message identity. Task-specific classifier analysis is blocked until suitable data and validation exist. Registration grants no execution authority. The four shared modes await admin selection.

Six roles account for earlier requests for darknet-related research, nuclear/critical-infrastructure scenarios and “black hole” concepts: Dark Signal/Nightwatch, Fission/Containment and Event Horizon/Sinkhole. These are bounded evidence-review and synthetic-simulation contracts. Darknet-related work uses lawfully obtained, redacted public reporting; critical-infrastructure scenarios are offline and nonoperational; graph simulations do not execute or propagate anything. These registrations do not create trained weights. CRUCIBLE was also requested as an independent review role and is now registered.

Earlier requests concerning mobile servers/apps, physical access, privacy/OPSEC and red/blue PoC review are represented by Nomad/Anchor, Worldline/Continuum, Threshold/Gatekeeper, Iron Ghost/Root of Trust, Trojan Dock/Portwarden, Obsidian and PoC · Red/PoC · Blue; they do not require duplicate roles.

The shared on-demand CISA feed supplies public vulnerability evidence. It does not supply role-specific labels for every domain or retrain weights. Downloaded tool references remain snapshots. Generated text is not an observation.

SCADA and satellite roles currently review supplied references/configuration evidence only. No live control-system, vehicle, spacecraft, RF-transmission or ground-station executor or task-specific trained head was added.

The junior OSINT role performs reference research and reports to Analyst. Specific God’s Eye View and GeoSentinel repository identities remain unconfirmed. The separate PoC candidate trainer requires admitted genuine observations and does not make the PoC role contracts independently trained or promoted.

Hierarchy: human admin → coordinator → leads → specialists → junior OSINT. The hierarchy routes proposals only; every implemented active operation retains exact scoped approval.

Malware Lab · Red / Malware Lab · Blue provide offline behavior review, harmless control-test planning and defensive triage. The referenced rootkit link catalog is metadata only; no samples, executors or trained weights were imported. See docs/MALWARE_LAB_ROLE_BOUNDARIES.md.
